What's inside

Five tabs. Most weeks you only touch the first two. The numbers below are sample data so you can see how it fills in.

Money In

One row per payment

Date, where it came from, the amount. Add miles if you drove. It suggests a set-aside for each payment (about 12% by default), or you can pick your own percentage in My Settings.

Money Out

Expenses and receipts

Supplies, platform fees, software, your share of the phone bill. Pick a category, tick the box once you've saved the receipt, and it comes off your profit automatically.

Quarterly

What to pay, and by when

The IRS due dates are uneven (Apr 15, Jun 15, Sep 15, Jan 15). This tab shows what you owe so far for each one and the amount still left to pay. A monthly checkbox reminds you to actually move the money to savings.

Year-End

A summary for tax time

Income, expenses, business miles and the mileage deduction, your profit, an estimate of self-employment and state tax, and how much came from each source. Bring it to whoever does your taxes, or use it to do your own.

Dashboard

Plus a little motivation

Logging a payment, saving a receipt and paying on time earn Grind Points. You rank up from Seed as you keep a streak going. It sounds silly, but it's what gets people to keep the sheet up to date.

Questions

Do I need Excel or a paid app?

No. It's a Google Sheet. You need a free Google account. After you buy, you'll get a link that makes your own copy in your Drive.

Is this tax advice?

No. Grindbook gives estimates to help you plan and save. Everyone's situation is different, so check with a tax professional for yours.

Can I change the set-aside percentage?

Yes. It starts at about 12%. Change it in My Settings and every row updates.

Does it connect to my bank or apps?

No, and that's on purpose. Nothing is linked and no one else sees your numbers. You type in what you earned.
