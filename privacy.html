Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 26, 2026

This policy covers the Grindbook website and Grindbook HQ, a small internal tool Grindbook uses to schedule its own marketing pins on Pinterest.

Who we are

Grindbook is a small business (an SJ Holdings brand) that sells a Google Sheets tax-tracker template on Etsy. Contact: grindbook.shop@gmail.com.

Grindbook HQ and Pinterest

Grindbook HQ connects only to Grindbook's own Pinterest business account. It is not offered to the public and no other Pinterest user can sign in to it. It is used to:

  • create and schedule pins on Grindbook's own boards, after the owner approves them;
  • read basic information about Grindbook's own account and boards (for example board names) so pins go to the right board;
  • read performance numbers for Grindbook's own pins (such as impressions and saves).

Grindbook HQ does not collect, store or sell data about other Pinterest users. The access token from Pinterest is stored privately on the owner's computer, is never shared, and can be revoked at any time from Pinterest's settings.

This website

The website has no accounts, forms, ads or tracking cookies. It is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may keep standard server logs (such as IP addresses) for security; see GitHub's privacy statement.

Etsy purchases

Purchases happen on Etsy. Etsy handles payment and shares only what sellers need to deliver the order, under Etsy's privacy policy. We use buyer details only to deliver and support the order.

Your choices

Email us to ask what we hold about you or to have it deleted. We reply within 30 days.

Changes

If this policy changes, we update the date above.
